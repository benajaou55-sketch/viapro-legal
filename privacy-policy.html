Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Preamble

This Privacy Policy describes how the Savevia Pro mobile application ("the Application"), developed by Benashe Softworks ("the Studio"), processes information. By using Savevia Pro, you agree to the practices described in this document.

Savevia Pro is a free, ad-supported Android application that allows downloading media from many websites and social platforms, saving temporary status or story content shared via messaging applications, converting video files into audio files, converting photos into PDF documents, compressing PDF files and videos, generating AI-powered summaries of YouTube videos, and securely storing personal files in a local, PIN or password-protected Vault. Savevia Pro is distributed outside Google Play.

Article 1 — Data Controller Identity

Studio: Benashe Softworks
Contact email: benashesoftworks@gmail.com
Capacity: Independent studio, sole data controller for the Savevia Pro Application.

Article 2 — Overview of Data Processing

2.1 No account

No account, name, email address or phone number is required to use the Application. The Studio does not operate user profiles.

2.2 What stays on your device

Downloaded media, converted files, photos and PDF files you process, compressed videos, Vault files, download history and settings are stored only on your device. The Studio has no access to them.

2.3 What may leave your device

  • the links (URLs) you paste to download media from some platforms, which are sent to the Studio's server for processing (Article 5);
  • if you choose to sign in to a platform inside the Application, the session cookies of that sign-in, which may be sent together with your request (Article 5.4);
  • direct requests from your device to the websites you download from (Article 5.1);
  • update checks for the embedded download components (Article 6);
  • if you use the YouTube Summary feature, the video identifier and public caption text (Article 11);
  • advertising requests and related technical data handled by Unity Ads (Article 7).

2.3 bis No payment data

Savevia Pro is free. It offers no paid tier, subscription or in-app purchase and does not collect any payment or financial information.

2.4 Local advertising-frequency data

To avoid showing too many advertisements, the Application keeps a few counters on the device only (number of actions and ads of the day, time of the last full-screen ad, session start). They are not linked to any identity, are not transmitted, and are deleted when the Application is uninstalled or its data is cleared.

2.5 No other analytics

Apart from the advertising SDK described in Article 7, the Application does not include any third-party analytics, profiling or crash-reporting SDK.

Article 3 — Local Features (No Upload)

3.1 Status/story saving

The Application reads the local storage directory used by the relevant messaging application, strictly limited to temporary status/story files, and copies the files you choose to your device storage. Nothing is transmitted to a server.

3.2 Video-to-audio conversion

The audio track is extracted from a video file already on your device. Conversion is performed entirely on the device.

3.3 Photo to PDF

You select photos through the Android system photo picker; the Application only accesses the photos you select. The PDF is generated on the device. Where a document scanner is offered, it uses Google's ML Kit Document Scanner through Google Play services, which runs on the device; Google may process diagnostic information according to its own policies.

3.4 PDF Compression and Video Compression

You select the file through the Android system document picker. Processing is performed on the device and the result is saved to your device storage. No PDF, video or part of its content is uploaded to any server or shared with any advertising network.

Article 4 — Vault: Local Secure Storage

The Vault moves photos, videos, audio and documents into a protected space inside the Application.

4.1 Local-only storage

Vault files are encrypted and stored only in the device's local memory. No Vault content is transmitted to the Studio or to any third party.

4.2 Access protection

Access is protected by a PIN code or a password, verified locally through a cryptographic hash, with secrets protected by the Android Keystore where supported. Repeated wrong attempts trigger a temporary lockout. Biometric unlock, where offered, is handled by the Android system; the Application never receives your biometric data.

4.3 No recovery by the Studio

  • the Studio cannot recover, reset or bypass a forgotten PIN or password;
  • loss of the PIN/password may result in permanent loss of access to Vault files;
  • you are solely responsible for remembering your credential and keeping independent backups of important files.

The Vault is a local protection tool, not a backup or cloud storage service.

Article 5 — How Downloads Are Processed

5.1 Processing on your device

For many websites supported by the embedded download engine, the link is analysed directly on your device and the media is downloaded directly from the source website. In this case the link does not pass through the Studio's server. The source website (and the companies behind it) can see your IP address and the technical information any app or browser sends, as it would when you visit the site normally. Its own privacy policy applies.

5.2 Processing by the Studio's server

For some platforms, the link you paste is sent to a server operated by the Studio, which finds the media address and returns it to the Application. The server receives the link, the requested format, and the technical data of any internet request (such as your IP address and a timestamp). The media file itself is not stored by the Studio. The server is hosted by Render (Render Services, Inc.), and may therefore be located outside your country.

5.3 Third-party services used by the server

To retrieve media, the server may route requests through proxy services and may forward the public link to third-party extraction services or to the platform itself. Those parties may see the link and the technical data of the request made by the server. The Studio does not send them your name, account or device identifiers.

5.4 Optional sign-in and cookies

Some content can only be retrieved with an authenticated session. If you choose to sign in to a platform through the sign-in screen of the Application, the sign-in takes place on the platform's own page displayed in the Application. The Application does not read or store your password. The session cookies created by the platform may be kept in the Application's web view on your device, and may be sent to the Studio's server together with your request so that the server can access the content on your behalf. They are used only to process your request, are never sold or used for advertising, and are not shared except as needed to contact the platform concerned. You can sign out or clear the Application's data at any time to remove them from your device.

Only sign in if you accept this. Signing in is never required to use the rest of the Application. Using your account with an unofficial tool may be against the platform's own terms and could lead to restrictions on your account; this is your decision and your responsibility.

5.5 Server logs and temporary cache

The server and its hosting platform may keep technical logs (links requested, IP address, time, error messages). To reduce repeated work, results of a link may be kept in a short-lived temporary cache, and request counters per IP address may be kept in memory to limit abuse. The Studio does not use these data to build profiles or to identify you, and does not sell them.

Article 6 — Updates of the Download Engine

The embedded download engine (yt-dlp) and its extraction components (including NewPipe Extractor) are open-source tools. To keep downloads working, the Application may check for and download updates of the engine in the background, which requires an internet connection and reveals your IP address to the server hosting the update.

Article 7 — Advertising (Unity Ads)

Savevia Pro is free and funded by advertisements. There is no paid or ad-free version.

7.1 Advertising provider

Advertisements are served by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies and its affiliates). Unity acts as an independent controller for the data it collects through its SDK. Its practices are described at https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

7.2 Types of ads

Banner ads, full-screen (interstitial) ads shown occasionally between two actions, and rewarded video ads that may be shown before some features.

7.3 Data processed by the advertising SDK

To serve, measure and secure ads, Unity Ads may collect or use the Android Advertising ID, the IP address (approximate location), device and operating-system information, and information about your interactions with ads. Depending on your location and choices, ads may be personalized or not.

No downloaded media, Vault content, photo, PDF file, video or link you paste is ever shared with the advertising network by the Application.

7.4 Consent and your choices

  • where required by law (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and certain US states), the Application asks for your choice before personalized advertising data is used; you can change it later from the Application's settings where available;
  • you can limit ad personalization or reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads);
  • limiting personalization does not remove ads; it only makes them less targeted.

Article 8 — Required Android Permissions

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: downloads, server requests, update checks, ads and online features;
  • AD_ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): allows the advertising SDK to access the Advertising ID;
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: download, conversion and compression progress notifications;
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (older Android versions only): saving media to the device storage.

Photos, videos and PDF files are chosen through the Android system pickers, which grant access only to the specific files selected. Files created by the Application are saved through Android's standard media storage API.

Article 9 — Children

Savevia Pro is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age set by the law of their country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided information, contact us at benashesoftworks@gmail.com.

Article 10 — Your Rights

Depending on your country, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection and portability regarding personal data that the Studio or its service providers may hold (for example server logs containing your IP address). Contact benashesoftworks@gmail.com. Because the Studio holds no account and cannot identify you from your usage, it may ask you for the approximate date and the link concerned to locate a request. For data held by Unity, Google or the websites you download from, exercise your rights with those companies.

Article 11 — AI-Powered Feature: YouTube Video Summary

11.1 What this feature does

This optional feature generates a written summary and a text transcript of a YouTube video you choose, using Google's Gemini API. You submit a YouTube URL; the public captions/subtitles of the video are retrieved and sent to Google's Gemini API for processing, through the Studio's server. No video or audio file is transmitted for this feature.

11.2 Data sent

The video identifier and its public caption text. The Application does not send your name, account or device identifiers for this feature. The Studio's server receives the technical data of the request (such as your IP address) as described in Article 5.5.

11.3 Google as a sub-processor

The Studio currently uses the free tier of the Gemini API. Under Google's terms for this tier, submitted text and generated responses may be used by Google to improve its products and to train and evaluate its models. If you do not accept this, do not use this feature.

11.4 Retention

To avoid repeated processing, the Studio's server may keep the transcript and summary in a cache for up to 30 days. Google retains its own request logs according to its own practices.

11.5 Consent and accuracy

The feature is not required to use the rest of the Application and requires your explicit consent before any caption text is sent. Summaries are generated by an AI model and may be inaccurate; see the Terms of Use.
